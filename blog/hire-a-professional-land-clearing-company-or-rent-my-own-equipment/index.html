Hire a Professional Land Clearing Company or Rent My Own Equipment?

February 26, 2025 · The Yard Force

When you are ready to develop property in the Knoxville, TN, region, you might need to clear the lot first. You have the option of renting equipment to do the job yourself or hiring a professional land clearing company.

How do you decide which option is right for you? Learn more about how to make the smartest decision for your situation.

Who Should Rent Equipment?

Some property owners want to try to clear their land themselves. For those who need to save money on the site development project, a DIY process is worth considering. Generally, renting equipment and putting in some sweat equity can reduce the project’s cost.

However, before renting power equipment for this process, you should have some experience with heavy equipment operation. Trying to learn how to safely use big equipment around large trees can be a dangerous combination.

If you want to do the job yourself, you should know how to drive a truck and trailer, as you’ll have to transport the heavy equipment to your job site. Some rental companies will transport and pick up the equipment for you, which may be helpful.

Ultimately, you probably shouldn’t rent equipment and try to do the work yourself without some experience. If the site has large trees or ravines, experience is important for doing the work safely.

Who Should Hire the Job Out?

Novices who have never done land clearing previously will want to hire our professionals. However, other important reasons exist for hiring a professional company.

When You Have Large Trees

Extremely large trees bring danger into the process. Cutting down trees with a large chainsaw can be physically tiring. 

Failing to deploy proper techniques when removing trees could put you or nearby structures in danger. Our professionals have the equipment to do the work properly and safely.

If You Need the Job Done Quickly

If you have a tight deadline to meet for completing the job, our professionals can give you a specific timeline. Just give our team the land surveying results that show the property lines and the areas you want cleared.

We’ll arrive on time on the promised day to start the work. Our experienced professionals will create accurate estimates for the time the job will take. If you’re on a tight deadline, we can bring more equipment and personnel to finish faster.

Receive Guarantees About the Work

When you need the job done perfectly, relying on our professionals is the smarter option. We follow your directions precisely for accurate work.

If you try to do the land clearing work yourself with rental equipment, you would have to rent the equipment a second time if you miss any areas. This drives up costs. 

Disposing of the Debris Can Require Help

When you hire our professionals, we can handle the debris in any manner you need. When doing the work yourself, you might struggle to haul debris off the site. 

Our team can mulch the trees and branches on-site for you. If you prefer, we could haul the debris to an off-site location, giving you a clean building site.

Other Considerations for Hiring Professionals or Doing a DIY Job

A few other considerations exist for deciding how to clear a piece of property.

Do You Have Nearby Infrastructure?

One key consideration for whether to do land clearing yourself or to hire a pro is the proximity of infrastructure. If the property has power lines, water lines, or sewer lines, you don’t want to inadvertently damage these items. If you hire a professional, you’ll receive insurance protecting against damage.

If you’re clearing property near your existing home or outbuildings, a professional will keep these structures safe.

Are You Worried About Fires?

If you’re clearing a piece of property with dry vegetation and old downed trees, a spark from land clearing equipment could start a fire. When you hire professionals, they will take steps for fire prevention.

Does the Property Have Environmental Concerns?

If you are unsure whether you need to follow environmental regulations, professionals can help you remain within the law. They’ll use equipment and techniques that protect parts of the property where environmental concerns exist.
