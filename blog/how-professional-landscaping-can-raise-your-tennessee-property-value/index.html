How Professional Landscaping Can Raise Your Tennessee Property Value

December 30, 2024 · The Yard Force

Tennessee homeowners expect investments they make in improving their property to add to its bottom line value over time. While home improvement projects often focus on interior renovations and additions, you shouldn’t overlook the positive impact professional landscaping can have on both value and long-term enjoyment. 

The Yard Force is the leading provider of expert landscape design services in the Knoxville area, and in this post we share some important insights on the positive impacts our work can have on your house’s market value.

How Professional Landscaping Can Transform Your Property

There’s much more to professional landscaping than mowing lawns and planting flowers. The work involves designing a cohesive and functional outdoor environment that complements your home’s architecture and the surrounding area. In East Tennessee, our properties typically feature diverse vegetation and rolling hills, meaning a “one-size-fits-all” approach never produces the best results.  

Hardscaping: Adding Structure and Functionality

Hardscaping involves permanent elements like patios, decks, and retaining walls that provide structure and utility to your property. 

Retaining walls are ideal for many local Knoxville properties with uneven terrain. They prevent erosion and increase usable yard space for terraced gardens and outdoor living spaces.

Garden and pathway designs support hardscaping features like pools, outdoor kitchens, and pergolas that form secure, peaceful spaces for outdoor entertaining or relaxation.

By combining form and function, hardscaping elements turn your outdoor space into a practical and inviting area for relaxation and entertainment.

Softscaping: Adding Beauty and Life

Softscaping involves the living elements of landscaping such as plants, trees, and flower beds. When carefully chosen, healthy, attractive plants will:

  • Highlight attractive architectural features of your home 
  • Create a harmonious natural aesthetic 
  • Attract pollinators like butterflies and bees to support your local ecosystem 

Native Tennessee plants, such as dogwoods and azaleas, thrive in the local climate and require minimal maintenance, making them ideal choices for a sustainable and beautiful landscape.

Shrub Trimming and Lawn Maintenance

Regular shrub trimming provides sharp-looking greenery that also prevents overgrowth that blocks windows and pathways. Regular lawn care and trimming is a simple baseline that boosts curb appeal and shows that you take good care of your property. 

How Landscaping Can Raise Your Property Value

Studies and real-life experience consistently show that professional landscaping provides a significant boost to property values. The American Society of Landscape Architects (ASLA) reports that high-quality landscaping can add 10% to 20% to a home’s value.  

Curb Appeal: First Impressions Matter

Potential buyers often form their first impression before they step inside a home, or just keep driving. A lush, healthy lawn, colorful flower beds, and clean pathway designs create an inviting look that immediately boosts interest.

Increased Usable Space

Hardscaping features like patios, decks, and fire pits directly expand your living space into the outdoors. These areas are highly desirable for entertaining guests or simply enjoying Tennessee’s seasonal weather. Properties with defined and useful outdoor spaces often sell faster and at higher prices.

Energy Efficiency and Long-Term Savings

Strategically placed trees and shrubs can lower energy costs by providing shade during the summer and blocking wind in the winter. Eco-friendly landscaping designs appeal to environmentally conscious buyers and everyone with an appreciation for natural beauty. 

Real Estate Insights

Realtors frequently recommend landscaping improvements before listing a home. According to the National Association of Realtors (NAR), 92% of realtors suggest improving curb appeal, and landscaping maintenance provides a whopping 104% return on investment (ROI).  

Consider the Long-Term Financial Benefits of Landscaping

Beyond the immediate increase in property value, professional landscaping offers multiple long-term financial benefits.

Low-Maintenance Upgrades

Work with landscaping professionals to ensure your improvements include durable materials and plants well-suited to our East Tennessee climate. Using native plants reduces the need for frequent watering and maintenance. Also, properly designed and installed hardscaping features require little upkeep to remain attractive and robust.

Reduced Erosion and Drainage Issues

Features like retaining walls and proper grading prevent soil erosion and facilitate effective drainage of stormwater and runoff — all of which protect soil surfaces and your home’s foundation. Effective landscaping reduces the risk of water damage and even helps to control pest infestation.  

Enjoyment Before You Sell

Even if you’re not planning to sell immediately, professional landscaping allows you to enjoy a more comfortable and attractive outdoor space for many years. The benefits are immediate and long-lasting.

Your property deserves the best care and attention, and professional landscaping can make that happen. The Yard Force team of experts specializes in creating outdoor spaces that combine beauty, functionality, and lasting value.
