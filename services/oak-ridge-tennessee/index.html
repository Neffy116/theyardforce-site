Serving Oak Ridge’s Landscaping Needs For Over a Decade

Land clearing and landscaping in Oak Ridge come with their own set of considerations. The city’s unique layout — planned in the 1940s and expanded over decades — means you’ll find everything from dense mature hardwoods buffering established neighborhoods to newer developments with raw land still waiting to be tamed. The terrain between the ridges can be steep, drainage can be tricky, and the Clinch River watershed means responsible land management matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve Anderson County and the Oak Ridge area?

Yes — Anderson County is a core part of our service territory. We regularly work in Oak Ridge, Oliver Springs, Clinton, and the surrounding communities. If you’re in Anderson County, we’re your team.

My Oak Ridge property has large, old trees and heavy undergrowth. Can you handle it?

That’s exactly what we do. Oak Ridge’s older neighborhoods often have significant tree canopy and years of accumulated undergrowth beneath it. Our forestry mulching equipment processes trees and brush of virtually any size, clearing the land cleanly without leaving behind the mess of traditional clearing methods. We’ll transform even the most overgrown lots.

Can you help with drainage issues on my Oak Ridge property?

Absolutely. Drainage is a common challenge in Oak Ridge’s valley terrain. Between grading, retaining walls, and smart landscape design, we can address standing water, erosion, and runoff issues that cause headaches for homeowners. We’ll assess your drainage situation during your free consultation.
