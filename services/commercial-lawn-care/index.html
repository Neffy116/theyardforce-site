Commercial Lawn Care FAQs

What types of commercial properties do you maintain?

Strip malls and retail centers, apartment complexes, churches, storage facilities, banks, office complexes, health centers and HOA common areas throughout the Knoxville area.

How are commercial contracts billed?

We total every agreed service for the year and divide it into 12 equal monthly invoices, so your landscaping budget is predictable.

What is included?

Typically mowing, trimming, edging, blowing, mulch, bed maintenance, pruning, leaf removal and irrigation checks. Every agreement is customized to the property.

Are you insured?

Yes. The Yard Force is fully insured.

Do you provide free bids?

Yes. We walk the property with you, then send a written scope and 12-month price at no cost. Call (865) 290-8815 or use the bid form on this page.
