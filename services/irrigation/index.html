Benefits of Professional Irrigation Services

Irrigation services play a key role in ensuring that your lawn looks beautiful for years to come. When done right, an irrigation installation will set the stage for a lush and beautiful lawn.

Working Smarter, Not Harder

The upfront costs of an irrigation system pay dividends for years to come as the watering work is automated. No need to move sprinklers around or get out your hose.

Professional Maintenance

If an irrigation system is not maintained, it can lose its efficiency. Sprinkler heads get out of adjustment, zones can be malfunctioning, water pressure can change. We do installation and then we offer a yearly maintenance plan to make sure the system is functioning at its peak performance.

Winterizing Your System

To winterize your system, our team will blow out the lines, make sure that the valves are shut off, make sure that there’s no water standing in the pipes that would freeze and break them over the course of the winter.

Frequently Asked Questions About Irrigation Services

Do you still have questions about our irrigation services? Find out more below.

Does the Yard Force install irrigation systems?

Yes, our team can work with you to plan and install an irrigation system on your property.

Is the up front cost of an irrigation system worth it?

Service calls are a $95 trip charge plus parts and labor, and labor starts at $150 per hour for two techs on site. A new multi-zone system installation can range from $5,000 to $15,000 or more depending on the number of zones, the type of controller and backflow.

These are wide, ballpark ranges, not a quote. Multiple factors affect the final price, including density, terrain and grade, access points to the site, the scope of work, materials and location. For an in-depth quote, it’s best for us to come out and see the property in person, so we can make sure you’re not being overcharged.

Does the yard force service my irrigation system?

Yes, you can contact us for any irrigation service issues, or sign up for our yearly maintenance plan.
