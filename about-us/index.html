Our Story

Here at The Yard Force we are a full-service landscaping company that desires to display excellence, integrity, and reliability through superior work and attention to detail. Our goal is to make a powerful impact by creating and maintaining exceptional landscapes. We are committed to being a positive force for good in our community by making sure it’s not just about the grass and shrubs, but by setting a new industry standard in quality, and providing meaningful work and training opportunities for those wanting to better themselves.

The Yard Force was built on hard work, a strong work ethic, and a passion for creating and maintaining beautiful outdoor spaces. Founders Adam and Clay both grew up as farm boys, learning the value of hard work at an early age. Their experience in landscaping began when they were young and has grown into a shared commitment to delivering quality work and exceptional service.

Adam is a University of Tennessee graduate, U.S. Army veteran, and current member of the Air National Guard. Clay is a University of Virginia graduate who enjoys giving back through his volunteer work with his church and schools.

Together, they bring a strong work ethic and a passion for making every customer’s experience a great one.
